# 230 — Nested groups main-app UI

**Parent:** [221 — Collections & media (spec)](221-collections-media-spec.md)

**What to build:** The three main-app collections render two-level groups: a parent accordion with its direct items and indented child accordions. Child groups collapse independently, the parent collapses the whole subtree, counts reflect the subtree, and the ⋯ menu gains a one-level "Move into…" path so nesting is fully keyboard-operable. Deleting a parent explains that its subtree is ungrouped and every item stays.

**Amendment (user-reported, 2026-10-24): colour no longer moves a group.** The leading gutter used to be applied only to coloured groups (12 px, with an 8 px override on nested children), so a group's colour — and a coloured parent's inset inherited by the child sitting inside it — changed where the grip, chevron, title and cards landed. A neutral child under a coloured parent sat ~12 px left of a coloured child under the same parent, and a coloured group's body also carried 4 px of colour-only vertical padding and 8 px of right padding, so its cards started on a different baseline. The gutter is now reserved by every group (12 px in the main app, 8 px in the dock strip) and colour only decides whether the bar is painted in it; the colour-only body padding is gone. Regression pinned in `dockGroupLayout.test.ts` ("group geometry is colour-independent"): the base `.group` rule owns `--group-inset` and `padding-left`, and `.group--colored` may not set either, nor add body padding.
